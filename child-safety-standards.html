Child Safety Standards

App: NasbUlSaddat · Last updated: June 11, 2026

NasbUlSaddat has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards and measures we follow to keep children safe and to prevent, detect, and respond to such content and behaviour on our platform.

1. Adults-only service

NasbUlSaddat is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Creating an account requires verification with a valid National ID, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the app. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will remove it.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

We strictly prohibit any content, conduct, or communication that sexualises, exploits, endangers, or abuses children, including:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt to exploit a minor.
  • Trafficking, or promoting, encouraging, or facilitating the abuse of children.

Violations result in immediate removal of the content, permanent termination of the account, and reporting to the relevant authorities.

3. In-app reporting

Every user can report unsafe content or behaviour directly inside the app. Report and Block tools are available on every post, story, comment, message, and user profile. Reports about child safety are treated as the highest priority and reviewed by our moderation team.

You can also report a concern by email to support.nasbulsaddat@gmail.com.

4. How we respond

  • We review child safety reports promptly.
  • We remove violating content and terminate offending accounts.
  • We preserve relevant information and report apparent CSAM and child exploitation to the appropriate regional and national authorities, and to recognised child-protection bodies where applicable.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement in accordance with applicable law.

5. Compliance

We comply with all relevant child safety laws in the jurisdictions where the app is available, and we report to regional and national authorities as required. We review these standards regularly and update them as needed.

For child safety questions, reports, or compliance matters, our designated point of contact is:

Email: support.nasbulsaddat@gmail.com
