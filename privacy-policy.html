Privacy Policy

App: NasbUlSaddat · Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in simple words, what information the NasbUlSaddat app collects, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. What Data We Collect

To make the app work, we collect only the information we need:

  • Account info — your email address and password (your password is stored securely and we never see it in plain text).
  • Profile info — your name, phone number, city, occupation, profile photo, and any "about" text you choose to add.
  • Identity verification — photos of the front and back of your National ID card (CNIC), used only to confirm you are a real person. These are kept in a private, secure area, are never shown publicly, and are never visible to other users. See section 5 for full details.
  • Media uploads — the images and videos you choose to upload for posts, stories, chats, and your profile, plus any documents you attach in chat.
  • Content you create — posts, comments, reactions, stories, connections, and messages.
  • Device info — a push-notification token and basic device type, so we can send you notifications.

We do not use any analytics or advertising tools, and we do not collect your advertising ID or track your activity.

What other members can see: your public profile — name, profile photo, city, occupation, and "about" — plus anything you choose to share (posts, stories, comments). Your email, phone number and National ID are private and are never shown to other members.

2. Why We Collect It

Data Why we use it
Email & password To create your account and let you log in securely.
Profile & National ID To set up your profile and confirm you are a real person.
Media (images/videos) To let you share photos and videos in posts, stories, and chats.
Content & connections To show your posts and stories and power the social features.
Device token To send you push notifications (likes, comments, messages, etc.).

3. Permissions We Use

The app only asks for a permission at the moment you use a feature that needs it. You can say no, and the rest of the app still works.

  • Camera — so you can take a photo or video, and scan your National ID card during sign-up.
  • Photos & videos (gallery) — when you choose to upload an image or video, the app opens your device's built-in system photo picker. This grants access only to the specific items you select and does not require any photos or storage permission.
  • Notifications — so we can alert you about new messages, comments, reactions, and connection requests.
  • Internet — so the app can connect to our servers to load and save your content.

We do not access your camera, gallery, or location in the background. Access happens only when you tap to use a feature.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely with trusted service providers:

  • Supabase — stores your account, profile, posts, messages, and uploaded media (in secure cloud storage).
  • Firebase (Google) — used only to deliver push notifications to your device.

Data is sent over secure, encrypted connections. Your National ID images are kept in a private storage area that other users cannot access.

5. National ID (CNIC) Verification

During sign-up you are asked to scan the front and back of your National ID card (CNIC) so we can confirm you are a real person and keep the community trusted and safe. Here is exactly how we handle it:

  • Your CNIC images are uploaded over a secure, encrypted connection to a private storage area that is separate from your public profile and posts.
  • They are never shown publicly and never visible to other users anywhere in the app.
  • Only authorised members of our admin team can view them, and only for identity verification.
  • We use them only for identity verification — never for advertising or selling. We do not share them with any third parties, except our secure cloud storage provider (Supabase), which stores them on our behalf and under our instructions so the app can function.
  • They are permanently deleted when your account is deleted (see section 9).

6. Reporting & Blocking (Keeping You Safe)

NasbUlSaddat lets people share posts, stories, comments, and messages, so we give you built-in tools to stay in control of what you see:

  • Report — you can report a post, a comment, a story, a message, or a user if you see something abusive or against our rules. Reports are sent to our admin team for review, and we may remove content or take action on accounts that break the rules.
  • Block — you can block any user. Once blocked, they can no longer message you, and you stop seeing each other's posts, stories, and comments. You can manage and undo blocks any time from the Blocked accounts screen.

To submit a report we store who reported, what was reported, and the reason, so our team can review it. We use this only for safety and moderation.

7. Data Safety

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and we do not collect your advertising ID.
  • We only use your data to run and improve the app's features.
  • We use access controls so users can only see data they are allowed to see.

8. Your Rights

You are in control of your information. You can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your profile details at any time inside the app.
  • Request that we delete your data (see section 9).

9. How to Delete Your Account

You can ask us to delete your account in either of two ways:

  • In the app — open your profile menu and tap Delete account. This sends a deletion request to our admin team. You can keep using the app while it is being reviewed.
  • By email — send an email to support.nasbulsaddat@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete My Account".

Our admin team reviews every request, usually within 30 days. Once a request is approved, we permanently delete your account and all related personal data — including your profile, posts, comments, reactions, stories, messages, connections, uploaded photos and videos, and your National ID (CNIC) images. This cannot be undone.

10. Children's Privacy

This app is intended only for adults aged 18 and over, and account creation requires a valid National ID. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please check back now and then to stay informed. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
